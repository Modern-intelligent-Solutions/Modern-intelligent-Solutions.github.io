Privacy Policy — WhatsApp Link Generator by ALLO

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This privacy policy describes how the Chrome extension “WhatsApp Link Generator by ALLO” (the “Extension”), published by Modern Intelligent Solutions (ALLO AI), handles information.

Summary

The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account, no login, and requests no browser permissions.

What the Extension does with your input

When you type a phone number and an optional message into the Extension’s popup, that input is used only on your own device to generate a WhatsApp link and an optional website button. This information is never sent to us, to any server, or to any third party, and it is not stored after you close the popup.

Data collection

None. The Extension does not collect personally identifiable information, health, financial, or authentication information, personal communications, location, web history, user activity, or website content.

Third parties & remote code

The Extension makes no network requests, loads no remote code, and includes no analytics or advertising. All code runs locally within the Extension package. Links you choose to open are handled by your browser and by WhatsApp under their own terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “last updated” date.
